## 英文提示词
A single cinematic scene inside a quiet theatre before a performance, evoking the feeling of being beside a dreamlike sea at twilight. A vast hand-painted theatrical seascape cloth fills most of the background: a spacious, nearly level horizon, calm distant water, restrained wave patterns, soft blue-green air and pearly evening light. The painted sea feels expansive and enveloping.

In the lower-right area, one adult stage scenery worker in plain, worn work clothes sits on a low stool, leaning slightly forward, looking down with relaxed concentration. The lower-right edge of this SAME hanging seascape cloth is drawn forward onto a low worktable directly in front of the worker. Its painted surface remains visibly continuous from the distant seascape to the folded fabric beneath the worker’s hands. The worker is mending a small tear in this supported section: one hand steadies the fabric while the other gently draws a short sewing thread through it; a small fabric patch and a few existing stitches help make the repair readable.

View the scene from the front of the painted cloth at a slight angle, in a medium-wide composition. Keep the seated figure large enough for the posture, hands and repair to remain legible, while leaving generous space for the sea. The cloth hangs mostly flat; only the nearby pulled-forward edge folds enough to reveal its woven texture and physical weight. A narrow strip of dry wooden stage floor and one discreet suspension tie at the far side quietly reveal the theatre.

Soft, broad stage lighting imitates coastal twilight, with a consistent light direction across the painted sea, the worker and the worktable. Muted cyan, grey-green and subtle warm pearly highlights, gentle atmospheric depth, clear repair area, believable anatomy and grounded proportions, tactile painted fabric. Quiet, intimate, gently dreamlike. One coherent physical space: the sea is painted scenery, and the worker remains on the dry stage.

## 中文对应说明
主要观看感受：仿佛人在梦幻暮色海边；仔细看能辨认舞台与修补。
空间：海景布在后方大面积悬挂，同一块布的右下缘向前拉至低台；人物在右下区域坐着修补，布料连接必须看得清。
动作：一手稳布，另一手牵短线；用裂口、补片和已成针脚共同提示修补，不把可读性只押在一根细针上。
材质：远处海景连贯，近处布边有少量折叠与织物质地；舞台地面干燥、有支撑。
光色：舞台试光模拟海边暮色；人物与背景光向一致，青蓝/灰绿与微暖珍珠色亮部，柔和空气感，工作处保持清楚。

## 首轮检查点
1. 海边氛围与修补动作能否同时读到。
2. 手下的布是否确实连续连接背景海景；是否被误画成修衣服。
3. 人、桌、凳和布是否同尺且有支撑。
4. 海景是否仍有辽阔感；舞台痕迹是否恰好可发现。
提示词只表达设计意图，不保证生成逐项遵守；取得结果后按实际画面逐项核对，再修改具体偏差。
